# Official scam types and red flags (research subagent, 19 Nov 2026; quotes as fetched)
RBI pages blocked automated fetch; BE(A)WARE quotes are from the Ombudsman edition mirrored at
https://taxguru.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/A-Booklet-on-Modus-Operandi-of-Financial-Fraudsters.pdf (RBI release 7 Mar 2022: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=53372, not opened).
PIB/TRAI/Sanchar Saathi read directly.

- Digital arrest (PIB 14 May 2024, PRID=2020570): "victims are made to undergo 'Digital Arrest' and remain visually available over Skype or other video conferencing platform to the fraudsters, till their demands are met."
- TRAI advisory (PIB 6 Aug 2025, PRID=2153153): "No regulatory body conducts investigations or collects payments through phone calls, messaging apps, or video platforms."
- Mobile disconnection (PIB 7 Apr 2025, PRID=2119782): "TRAI does not initiate communication with Customers regarding Mobile Number disconnection through messages or otherwise"; DoT (PIB 14 May 2024, PRID=2020557): "DoT does not make calls to citizens threatening disconnection".
- PIN to receive money (BE(A)WARE p.5): "Always remember, to receive money there is no need to enter your PIN / password anywhere."
- QR scan (BE(A)WARE p.11): "Fraudsters often contact customers under various pretext and trick them into scanning QR codes using payment apps."
- Screen sharing (BE(A)WARE p.8): "Do not download or activate share screen share feature with unknown people."
- Vishing (BE(A)WARE p.4): "Bank officials / financial institutions / any genuine entity never ask customers to share confidential information such as username / password / card details / CVV / OTP."
- Fake customer care (BE(A)WARE p.10): "Avoid searching for customer care contact details on search engine."
- KYC expiry (Chakshu, PIB 22 May 2024, PRID=2021309): "...with the intention of defrauding like KYC expiry or update of bank account/payment wallet/SIM/gas connection/electricity connection..."
- Job fraud (BE(A)WARE p.15): "Always remember that a genuine company offering job will never ask for money."
- SIM swap (BE(A)WARE p.9): "Never share credentials pertaining to SIM card."
- Lottery (BE(A)WARE p.14): "Do not make payments or share secure credentials for lottery calls / emails."
- Impersonation (BE(A)WARE p.12): "Always verify genuineness of fund request with the friend / relative or confirm by a phone call / physical meeting..."
- Chakshu (https://sancharsaathi.gov.in/sfc/): "Chakshu facilitates citizens to report the suspected fraud communications." "Report within 30 days for action." Attempted (no loss) → Chakshu; actual loss → I4C (1930) (PIB 5 Feb 2026, PRID=2223779).
